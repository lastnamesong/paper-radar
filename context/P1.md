## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: **tactile sensors** (vision-based, array and e-skin), **vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) policies**, and **dexterous manipulation** with multi-fingered hands, including in-hand manipulation and the datasets and benchmarks they use. A paper lands here when touch or finger-level control is part of its claim.

**Thesis tie**: tactile and dexterous results are the robot-side counterpart of P0's body-side wearables — a paper is P1-relevant when it changes what a hand senses through contact or how it is controlled.

**Tracked items**: tactile modality (D4TM), tactile in the policy (D7VT).

**Scouting lens**: wide recall around tactile-conditioned policies; vision-only dexterous work is surfaced and scored, not rejected outright.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to
P1 or a P1 Decision):

- Gripper or parallel-jaw work with no finger-level or contact-sensing contribution
- Vision-only grasp-pose prediction
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)
