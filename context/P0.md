## 1. Scope [STABLE structure, LIVING content] [AGENT-INPUT]

**Owns**: **wearable robots and exoskeletons** (lower-limb, upper-limb, soft/exosuit, assistance control), **wearable sensors** (IMU, EMG, insole/pressure, flexible and e-skin sensing on the body), **human intent and gait estimation** from them, and **human biomechanics** (gait and joint kinematics and kinetics, musculoskeletal and neuromusculoskeletal modelling, joint torque and muscle force estimation, metabolic cost). A paper lands here when its object is a device worn on the body, the signal read from it, or the mechanics of the body that device assists.

**Thesis tie**: wearables are the body-side counterpart of the robot-side tactile and manipulation work in P1 — a paper is P0-relevant when it changes how a worn device senses or assists.

**Tracked items**: wearable actuation vs. sensing focus (D3WA), intent/gait estimation modality (D6KM).

**Scouting lens**: wide recall around assistance control, wearable sensing and human-movement biomechanics; clinical-only and industrial-ergonomics studies are surfaced and scored, not rejected outright.

**Anti-topics** (noise filter — excluded unless an unusually strong tie to
P0 or a P0 Decision):

- Pure clinical trials with no device or signal-processing contribution
- Consumer fitness-tracker validation studies
- Sports-performance biomechanics with no assistive, sensing or modelling contribution
- Survey / position papers (read manually, not via agent)
